## Contract and interpretation

The [#75 managed breakdown and Covenant forward contract](https://github.com/Retro-Downfall/RetroDownfall.Arcanum/issues/75), [#73 approved architecture update](https://github.com/Retro-Downfall/RetroDownfall.Arcanum/issues/73), and [OATH §20.4](../Arcanum.OATH.md#204-the-long-rest-75-epic) refine the earlier proposal. Owning contracts are [DESIGN §21.16–21.16.6 and §10.8.2](../Arcanum.DESIGN.md), [API §8.36 and memory inspection](../Arcanum.API.md), [Command Reference](../Arcanum.Command.Reference.md), and [Compendium configuration](../Compendium.README.md).

- Consolidation preserves immutable observations. Ten equivalent observations converge to **one effective claim**, with ten retained exact input versions and nine suppressions; it does not overwrite ten source rows with one row.
- Similarity nominates candidates. The approved [#93 contract](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-long-rest-deterministic-consolidation.md) requires exact declarations and deterministic canonical policy. [#91](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-long-rest-hybrid-sweeps.md) adds bounded discovery and closed adjudication; [Carried Admission](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-13-long-rest-carried-admission.md) governs recovery. The original write-time near-neighbor mutation and arbitrary model conflict pass are not delivered behavior and must not be checked as such.
- The original “reinforcement count of ten” is superseded. Observation count, retrieval, admission and successful exposure are not positive usefulness credit. #95 requires eligible counterfactual evidence corroborated by committed successful exact-version use. Ten preserved observations do not imply ten positive credits.
- Background recovery parks work. Only the worker readmits clean work; a fresh authenticated request supplies protected authority. A daemon cannot borrow authority from a checkpoint.
- Deferred cross-generation paid reuse, debt thresholds, per-turn allowances and throughput optimizations remain outside this acceptance review. This record adds no feature or release criterion.
